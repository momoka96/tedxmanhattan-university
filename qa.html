01What is TEDxManhattan University?+

TEDxManhattan University is a student-driven TEDx event bringing together ideas, stories, and perspectives from our campus and beyond. It's a space for meaningful conversations and ideas worth spreading.

02When and where will the event take place?+

The event is planned for Spring 2027 at Manhattan University in Riverdale, New York. Additional event details will be announced as planning develops.

03How can I apply to speak or perform?+

Applications are available through our Apply to Speak form. We're looking for compelling ideas, perspectives, and performances that can inspire and engage an audience.

More information about joining the team will be announced soon. Our student organization includes executive leadership and departments focused on speakers, production & media, sponsorship & finance, and marketing & design.
